AI video tool notes

Short notes on three browser-based tools, written as a decision log rather than a review. No install, no affiliate links.

single shot

Wan 3.0

Text-to-video, image-to-video and motion control with presets. The presets are the actual product: pick a slow push-in or a handheld drift and the clip reads differently before you touch a prompt. This is the fastest of the three to a usable shot, and the one with the least timeline.

https://wan-3.io

full pipeline

Pavo AI

Generation, timeline editing, captions and multilingual dubbing share one workspace, which is the reason I stopped stitching three tabs together. The deciding feature was character consistency: lock the reference frame once and shot three still looks like shot one.

Order that works: storyboard, generate with a locked reference, cut, then dub. Dubbing before the cut is locked wastes a pass.

https://pavoai.app · https://pavoai.app/prompts

reference-driven

Rea Video

Video recreation: give it a clip you like, describe the new subject, and it re-shoots the scene with your subject in it. It does not invent a scene, which is exactly the point — it is a re-shooter, not a brainstorm.

https://reavideo.pro
